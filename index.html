How to order

Three simple steps

We sell personally, not through a checkout — every order starts with a conversation.

1

Find a piece you love

Browse the collection here, or see the newest pieces first on Instagram at @k_ceramic_canada.

2

Send us a DM

Message @k_ceramic_canada on Instagram with the piece you have in mind. We will confirm it is available and its price.

3

Pickup or shipping

Local pickup is in Langley, BC. If you need shipping, we will confirm the cost and payment with you in the same conversation.

About

Clay, colour, and patience

K-Ceramic Canada brings Korean handmade ceramics to Canadian homes from Langley, British Columbia. We work with motto, a colour-tableware studio in Bundang, Korea, and with visual artist Michelle Hong, whose ceramic and kintsugi works are exhibited in Korea and Canada.

For Michelle's full portfolio — painting, performance art and exhibitions — visit her artist site.
